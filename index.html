Voice chat

Hold V (d-pad up, or the Talk button on a phone) to talk to your team. The microphone only opens while you hold it.

Controls

Keyboard and mouse

  • Mouse aim · Click fire (on a ramp marker, pull three times quickly, then hold for 10 a second)
  • WASD move · Shift sprint
  • Space jump · C crouch · Z lie down (tap to stay down, or hold)
  • R reload · Tab scoreboard · V talk (hold)
  • H first / third person · Q swap shoulders · Esc pause

Controller

  • Left stick move · click it to sprint
  • Right stick aim · click it for first / third person · RT fire
  • A jump · B crouch (tap) or lie down (hold) · X reload
  • LB sprint · Select scoreboard · d-pad up talk · d-pad left / right shoulder
  • Start pause · in menus A picks, B goes back

Eliminate the whole other team to win the point. First to five points wins the match.
